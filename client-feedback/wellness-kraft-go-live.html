Phase 14

New in this build — priority re-test

These flows changed recently (Google-only login, gated booking, phone-once, required photos, drag-and-drop uploads, no seeded data). Re-test them specifically before launch — they aren't covered by earlier passes.

  • Google is the only login — no phone/OTP anywhere.BlockerSign-in and sign-up show only “Continue with Google”. No phone-number or OTP field appears on any auth screen.
  • Brand-new Google account creates itself.BlockerA never-before-seen Google account signs in first try and lands on the dashboard — no “external account not found”.
  • Signed-out visitor can’t reach the booking form.BlockerOn an expert with a Cal link, a logged-out visitor sees “Sign in to book with …” + Google button — never the payment/calendar directly.
  • After sign-in you return to the same expert.Signing in from the booking gate lands you back on that expert’s page, ready to book — not on a generic dashboard.
  • No name/email re-entry when signed in.The booking box shows “Booking as [name] · [email]” from Google — it never asks you to type name or email again.
  • Phone is asked once, then remembered.BlockerFirst booking asks for a phone; book again later and it’s pre-filled (with an “Edit” option) — not asked from scratch.
  • Paid consult: pay → calendar, as the signed-in user.BlockerFee pays with a test card, then the calendar opens pre-filled with your Google name/email.
  • Expert with no Cal link shows the safe fallback.An expert with no booking link shows “booking coming soon → Request a booking” (contact), not a broken widget or the gate.
  • Admin warns about un-bookable experts.The admin dashboard shows “N live experts have no booking link”; the expert form warns + confirms “save anyway?” when Live without a link.
  • A product can’t be saved without a photo.BlockerTrying to save a product with no image is blocked — in the form and if the API is called directly.
  • An expert can’t be saved without a photo.BlockerSame rule for experts — a photo is required to save.
  • Drag-and-drop upload + crop works.BlockerDragging a photo into a product/expert opens the crop window; “Crop & upload” stores it and shows the square thumbnail.
  • Uploaded images come out uniform.Products crop to 1000×1000, experts to 800×800 — the shop grid and expert cards look even, no stretched/odd-ratio photos.
  • Multiple product photos + main + remove.A product accepts several photos; the first is tagged “Main”; the × removes one; the shop shows the main photo.
  • Upload fallback is graceful.If the Blob token is missing, the picker says uploads aren’t on and lets you paste a link instead — never a dead end or crash.
  • Vercel Blob token set in production.Live-onlyBLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN is in Vercel and a real upload from the live admin succeeds.
  • No dummy data anywhere.BlockerShop and experts show only what you added in /admin. No sample “Priya Sharma” / “Ashwagandha” placeholders remain.
  • Empty catalog degrades cleanly.Before you add content, shop/experts/home show tidy empty states — no crash, no leftover demo data.

  • Unpublished items stay hidden.BlockerA product/expert with “Visible” unticked does not appear on the public site, only in /admin.
  • Detail pages never 404 on a DB hiccup.If the database briefly fails, an expert/product detail page falls back gracefully rather than showing “page doesn’t exist”.
  • Admin is locked in production.BlockerSince Google login is configured, /admin needs a real admin sign-in even in dev — the old “open in dev” bypass no longer applies.
