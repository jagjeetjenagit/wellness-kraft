# Homepage Image Prompt Sheet — Wellness Kraft

The homepage now has **graphic slots**. Each one shows a neat placeholder
until you drop in a real image, so the site never looks broken.

## How to use this sheet

1. Pick a slot below and copy its **Prompt** into your image-generation tool
   (Midjourney, DALL·E, Ideogram, Adobe Firefly, etc.).
2. Generate at (or near) the **Size / shape** shown.
3. Save the result with the **exact file name** into the folder:
   `public/graphics/`
4. Refresh the site — the image appears automatically. No code needed.

> Keep names lowercase and exactly as written (e.g. `hero.jpg`).
> JPG or PNG both work; JPG keeps the site fast. Aim for under ~500 KB each.

### If an image doesn't show up

- **Check the file name.** Image tools often save as PNG and add their own
  extension, giving you `hero.jpg.png`. The name must be exactly `hero.jpg`
  (not `hero.jpg.png`, not `Hero.JPG`). In Windows Explorer, turn on
  "File name extensions" (View menu) so you can see the real ending.
- **Check the size.** Raw AI images are often 5–8 MB, which is too heavy for a
  website. Keep each under ~500 KB. If yours are big, send them over and they
  can be shrunk automatically — no quality loss you'd notice.

## Slot 1 — Hero image

- **File name:** `hero.jpg`
- **Size / shape:** portrait, about **1200 × 1500 px** (4:5). It also crops
  nicely to landscape on small screens, so keep the subject centred.
- **Where it shows:** top of the homepage, right of the main headline.
- **What it should say:** this is the first thing visitors see, so it must
  convey **both** sides of the brand at once — a real expert *consultation*
  **and** the *natural products*. (The old herbs-only flat-lay showed products
  only and missed the consultation story.)

**Prompt (recommended — expert + products together):**

> Warm, authentic Indian wellness scene: a young female doctor (late 20s /
> early 30s, warm and friendly) wearing a clean white medical coat over
> smart-casual clothing, sitting at a light wooden table in a bright, calm
> clinic, warmly guiding a client. On the table between them, a tidy
> arrangement of natural wellness products — a few amber glass supplement jars,
> a small bowl of golden turmeric, fresh tulsi and herbs, a glass of herbal
> infusion. She is mid-conversation, reassuring and genuine. Soft natural
> daylight, shallow depth of field, earthy olive-green and cream palette with a
> touch of turmeric gold, generous breathing room, centred composition. Clean
> editorial photography, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Portrait 4:5.

**Alternative (single person, more product-forward):**

> Warm, premium Indian wellness portrait: a young female doctor (late 20s /
> early 30s), warm and approachable, wearing a clean white medical coat, gently
> presenting a natural supplement jar toward the viewer, with a soft-focus
> arrangement of Ayurvedic herbs, turmeric and a herbal infusion on the cream
> linen table beside her. Bright natural daylight, shallow depth of field, calm
> and trustworthy mood, centred composition, lots of breathing room. Clean
> editorial photography, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Portrait 4:5.

> Tip: whichever you pick, keep the person slightly off-centre or mid-frame so
> the layout crops well to landscape on phones. Generate a few and choose the
> one where the expert looks genuine (not stocky) and the products read clearly.

## Slot 2 — Approach image ("Why we're different")

- **File name:** `approach.jpg`
- **Size / shape:** landscape, about **1200 × 900 px** (4:3).
- **Where it shows:** middle of the homepage, beside "Guidance first.
  Products second."

**Prompt:**

> A warm, authentic moment of an Indian wellness expert (nutritionist or
> Ayurvedic doctor) in a bright, calm consultation space, attentively
> listening and explaining to a client across a wooden table with a few
> natural products and notes. Natural daylight, soft focus background, earthy
> olive-and-cream palette, genuine and reassuring mood. Editorial photography,
> no text, no logos, no watermarks. Landscape 4:3.

## Slot 3 — Wide lifestyle banner

- **File name:** `lifestyle-band.jpg`
- **Size / shape:** very wide, about **2100 × 900 px** (21:9). Keep important
  detail in the middle — the edges are trimmed on small screens.
- **Where it shows:** wide strip near the bottom of the homepage.

**Prompt:**

> Wide cinematic flat-lay banner of natural wellness ingredients arranged in a
> gentle horizontal line on a cream surface: ashwagandha root, turmeric,
> tulsi, moringa, honey, seeds and dried herbs, softly lit with natural
> daylight, calm and premium, earthy olive-green and cream palette with subtle
> turmeric-gold accents. Generous negative space, subject kept in the centre,
> clean editorial style, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Ultra-wide 21:9.
